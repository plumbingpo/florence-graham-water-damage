Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Florence-Graham, Florence-Graham Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Florence-Graham

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
